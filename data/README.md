## Policy

- Keep full raw datasets on Zenodo.
- Keep only small test or example data in git.
- Record archive names, checksums, and dataset provenance when new data files are added.
- Avoid committing regenerated caches, temporary extraction folders, or platform metadata.

## Manuscript consistency

The manuscript data-availability statement should point to the final Zenodo
record or DOI for this dataset. Before resubmission, verify that the manuscript
DOI resolves to the same record as the repository README.
